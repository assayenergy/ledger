# Track record

What I published, and what the record shows. Dated, so that you can check. This page lists
findings, not predictions. Assay documents what is already in the public record. It doesn't
forecast anything.

**July 31, 2026 — FlexValue published.**

Node-level pricing of grid flexibility across ten ERCOT nodes, from public price data.
Calibration — the model's implied all-in cost for a flexible load at the Odessa substation was
$26.31 per MWh for 2024. Cipher Mining, which operates a 207 MW facility at that same substation,
disclosed an all-in power price of about $27 per MWh in its Q1 2025 filings. The model, built
without any knowledge of their contracts, landed within about a dollar. The 2025 model year ran
higher ($36.55), and the reasons are stated in the piece.

**August 3, 2026 — Governor Abbott directs PUCT and ERCOT to verify every data center in the
interconnection process.**

It is listed because it is the backdrop for the next entry.

**August 4–5, 2026 — GridScore evidence audit run.**

Twenty-one announced Texas data center projects, about 28.7 GW of disclosed capacity, scored on
five public-record signals. Started before the directive, finished two days after it.

**August 9, 2026 — GridScore published.**

Findings that stand on the record:

- About 23% of disclosed announced capacity had no verifiable public evidence. No site control,
  no permits, no filings, nothing financial.
- The largest announcement in Texas, Fermi's 11 GW Project Matador, scored 65 and carried a
  documented-reversal flag, a terminated $150M tenant funding agreement, a one-day stock decline
  of about 33% in December 2025, and an open securities class action (Lupia v. Fermi, S.D.N.Y.).
  Assay did not predict the reversal, it documented it from the record, which is the point.
- Zero of 21 projects held a JETI agreement, and none of them actually could. Data centers are
  statutorily excluded.
- ERCOT's own June 2026 figures state that 8,926 MW was approved to energize and 3,966 MW was
  actually drawing power, against a request queue near 475 GW.
- No public mechanism exists to check an announced project against ERCOT's queue by name. The
  state's audit order and Assay's ledger exist because of the same gap.

**Open items I am watching.**
