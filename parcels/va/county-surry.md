# Surry County, VA — Solar & Fiscal Profile

> Disclosure: the author has a financial interest in land acquisition in the counties covered by
> this research.

As of 2026-09-22; precedent check and cap recomputation added 2026-09-23. Every fact is tagged `[gov-record]` (a county ordinance, Board of Supervisors
record, or SCC/state filing) or `[press]` (news reporting). Where a fact could not be sourced, it

## Solar ordinance

- **Original adoption:** December 6, 2018 (originally placed in Chapter 10, Environment; later
  recodified into the Zoning Ordinance). `[gov-record]` — recited in the [Feb. 13, 2025 Public
  Hearing Notice](https://surrycountyva.gov/DocumentCenter/View/1861/Public-Hearing-Notice---Board-of-Supervisors---Solar-Ordinance---21325).
- **Most recent amendment:** Board of Supervisors held a public hearing and voted **3–1 on
  February 13, 2025** to adopt PC Ordinance Amendment 2024-02 — recodifying the solar rules into
  the Zoning Ordinance (Appendix A) and tightening the acreage cap (see below). `[gov-record]`/`[press]`
  — draft ordinance (above); ["Surry supervisors vote 3-1 to enact 7% cap on solar
  farms," Smithfield Times, Feb. 19, 2025](https://www.smithfieldtimes.com/2025/02/19/surry-supervisors-vote-3-1-to-enact-7-cap-on-solar-farms/).
- **Source for the quotes in this section:** Surry County Code of Ordinances, Appendix A (Zoning), on
  Municode (online version labeled "Supplement 12"), captured 2026-09-23. `[gov-record]` Each
  section's history note is given with its quote.
- **Districts: never by-right, and only in M-1 or M-2.** § 1-501.1, last sentence:
  *"Such facilities are permitted only in the (M-1) Light Industrial and the (M-2) General
  Industrial Zoning Districts."* `[gov-record]` —
  [§ 1-501.1](https://library.municode.com/va/surry_county/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=PTIILADEOR_APXAZO_ARTIGEPR_1-500PLCOBOSU_S1-501.1COUSPECOALSOENFAUTALSOENFA)
  (Ord. No. 2025-01, § 1, 2-13-2025). The M-1 and M-2 use lists put both solar tiers under
  subsection C, which opens *"The following uses are allowed only by conditional use permit from the
  board of supervisors pursuant to section 1-501"*, and list *"Community-scale solar energy
  facility\*"* and *"Utility-scale solar energy facility\*"* there. `[gov-record]` —
  [§ 3-902(C)](https://library.municode.com/va/surry_county/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=PTIILADEOR_APXAZO_ARTIIIZODIBO_3-900GEINDI_S3-902PEUS) (Ord. No. 2025-01,
  § 1, 2-13-2025; Ord. No. 2025-02, 4-3-2025);
  [§ 3-1002(C)](https://library.municode.com/va/surry_county/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=PTIILADEOR_APXAZO_ARTIIIZODIBO_3-1000GEINDI_S3-1002PEUS) (same history, plus
  Ord. No. 2016-02, 8-4-2016). Municode's headings title both districts "General Industrial"
  (3-900 "M-1 General Industrial District"; 3-1000 "M-2 General Industrial District"), while
  § 1-501.1 calls M-1 "Light Industrial".
- **Agricultural districts: neither solar tier appears in any use list.**
  - **A-R, Agricultural-Rural (§ 3-302):** its by-right (A), special-exception (B) and
    conditional-use (C) lists contain neither "Community-scale solar energy facility" nor
    "Utility-scale solar energy facility". `[gov-record]` —
    [§ 3-302](https://library.municode.com/va/surry_county/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=PTIILADEOR_APXAZO_ARTIIIZODIBO_3-300AGRADI_S3-302PEUS) (Ord. No. 2013-01,
    9-5-2013; Ord. No. 2023-03, 2-2-2023; Ord. No. 2024-08, 12-5-2024; Ord. No. 2025-02,
    4-3-2025).
  - **A-L, Agricultural Limited (§ 3-202):** same result. `[gov-record]` —
    [§ 3-202](https://library.municode.com/va/surry_county/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=PTIILADEOR_APXAZO_ARTIIIZODIBO_3-200AGLIDI_S3-202PEUS) (Ord. No. 2013-01,
    9-5-2013; Ord. No. 2024-08, 12-5-2024).
  - **Note on A-R:** § 3-302(C) does list *"Power plant"* and *"Utility service/major\*"* as
    conditional uses. § 2-202 defines "Power plant" as *"An establishment that produces electricity
    for offsite distribution to the consumer."* **INFERENCE:** § 1-501.1's specific rule ("permitted
    only in" M-1/M-2) controls for solar facilities. No county record was found treating a solar
    facility as a "power plant" in A-R.
- **Setbacks, § 4-608(A)(7), verbatim:** *"The project area shall be set back at least 300 feet
  from all abutting public rights of way and main buildings on adjoining parcels and from adjacent
  property lines. Exceptions to these distances are possible for adjoining parcels owned or leased
  by the applicant."* `[gov-record]` —
  [§ 4-608](https://library.municode.com/va/surry_county/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=PTIILADEOR_APXAZO_ARTIVSURE_4-600SURECIUS_S4-608COALSOENFAUTALSOENFA)
  (Ord. No. 2025-01, § 1, 2-13-2025). **Measured from: both.** The 300 ft runs from adjacent
  property lines, from public rights-of-way, and from main buildings on adjoining parcels.
  - **The 300 ft setback runs from all adjacent property lines, so a parcel narrower than about
    600 ft (300 + 300) has no usable area.** The one exception in the text is where the
    adjoining parcels are owned or leased by the applicant.
- **Buffer, § 4-608(A)(13)(a), verbatim:** *"The buffer shall consist of a landscaping strip at
  least 300 feet wide, shall be located within the setbacks required, and shall circle the entire
  perimeter of the property."* `[gov-record]` (same source)
- **Waiver, § 4-608(B), verbatim opening:** *"In issuing any conditional use permit for a
  utility-scale solar generation facility, the board of supervisors may waive or modify any of the
  requirements of subsection (A) above"*. `[gov-record]` (same source) The text names
  utility-scale facilities only. **INFERENCE:** a community-scale (≤5 MW) facility has no express
  waiver of the 300 ft setback or buffer. Separately, § 4-608(A)(13)(f) lets the Planning
  Commission *"recommend waiving or altering the vegetative screening and/or buffer
  requirements"* where wetlands or woodlands are preserved. Neither provision has been applied to a
  community-scale project; none has been filed (see "Precedent check").
- **Acreage/percentage cap, § 1-501.1, verbatim (codified):** *"the board of supervisors
  determined that the total approved utility-scale solar generation facilities shall not exceed
  seven percent of developable land within the county or 10,695 acres in total. Land calculation is
  based on the entire acreage for parcel seeking a conditional use permit."* `[gov-record]`
  (Municode, above). This **replaced a 10%/15,278-acre cap** adopted in 2023. `[press]` —
  ["Surry County approves 10% cap on solar farms," Smithfield Times, April 10, 2023](https://www.smithfieldtimes.com/2023/04/10/surry-county-approves-10-cap-on-solar-farms/);
  ["Surry proposing stricter 7% cap, down from 10%," Smithfield Times, Oct. 30, 2024](https://www.smithfieldtimes.com/2024/10/30/surry-proposing-stricter-7-cap-on-solar-farms-down-from-10/).
- **Inter-project spacing rule:** **no numeric minimum distance.** § 4-608(B)(4) lists "The
  proximity of the site to other large scale solar energy facilities" only as a matter the Board
  considers when deciding a utility-scale waiver. `[gov-record]` (Municode, above)

Other ordinance provisions of note: facility centroid must be within one mile of existing
high-voltage transmission lines; a county-developer siting agreement is required; max height 18
ft; decommissioning bond = 100% of estimated cost + 20%. `[gov-record]` — §§ 1-501.1, 4-608(A).

## Does this apply at 5 MW

**APPLIES on CUP, rezoning and setback; AMBIGUOUS on the acreage cap.** Precedent-check verdict
(2026-09-23): **ADVERSE on current agricultural zoning**, with a rezoning path shown by two
utility-scale projects; see "Precedent check" below.

Surry's ordinance (Article II, Sec. 2-202, Use type descriptions) defines three size tiers,
quoted verbatim from [§ 2-202 on Municode](https://library.municode.com/va/surry_county/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=PTIILADEOR_APXAZO_ARTIIDEUS_2-200USTY_S2-202USTYDE)
(captured 2026-09-23; history ends "Ord. No. 2025-01, § 1, 2-13-2025; Ord. No. 2025-02, 4-3-2025"): `[gov-record]`

- *Distributed solar energy facility*: "An solar energy generation facility with a rated capacity
  less than [1] MWac. Distributed solar energy facilities are used to meet energy demands on-site
  and include rooftop and groundmounted photovoltaic arrays on residential, commercial, and
  institutional properties." (Treated as an accessory use — Sec. 4-204.A.4.) The Municode text
  reads "less than MWac" with the number missing; "[1]" is supplied from context.
- *Community-scale solar energy facility*: "An energy generation with a rated capacity greater
  than 1 MWac (megawatts alternating current) and no greater than 5 MWac. Facilities do not
  require connections to high voltage transmission lines."
- *Utility-scale solar energy facility*: "A solar energy generation facility with a rated capacity
  greater than 5 MWac."

**A project rated at exactly 5 MW AC falls at the top edge of "community-scale," not
"utility-scale"** (which requires *greater than* 5 MWac).

- **CUP requirement: APPLIES.** "Community-scale solar energy facility\*" is its own entry in the
  conditional-use lists of M-1 and M-2, with the same M-1/M-2-only restriction as utility-scale.
  §§ 3-902(C), 3-1002(C), 1-501.1. `[gov-record]` (Municode, above)
- **300-ft setback: APPLIES, from property lines as well as buildings and roads.** § 4-608 is
  titled *"Community-scale solar energy facility or utility-scale solar energy facility"* and opens
  *"Performance standards for community-scale solar energy facilities and utility-scale solar
  energy facilities."* `[gov-record]` The (A)(7) setback therefore binds a 5 MW project. The (B)
  waiver names only utility-scale (see "Solar ordinance" above).
- **7%/10,695-acre cap: AMBIGUOUS.** Sec. 1-501.1's heading also names both tiers together
  ("Conditional use permits for community-scale solar energy facilities or utility-scale solar
  energy facilities"), but the operative cap sentence itself uses only the narrower term: *"the
  total approved **utility-scale** solar generation facilities shall not exceed 7 percent of
  developable land within the County or 10,695 acres in total."* Surry's ordinance is otherwise
  careful to write "community-scale... or utility-scale..." every time it means to cover both (the
  §4-608 title itself, and its decommissioning subsections 2–3). The narrower wording in the cap
  sentence specifically is plausibly deliberate — meaning a 5 MW community-scale facility may
  require a CUP without counting against the 10,695-acre ceiling — but this reading has not been
  confirmed by any official interpretation, letter, or BOS record found. `[gov-record]`

  The Cavalier Solar approval (May 2021) does not resolve this. Cavalier is utility-scale at
  240 MW under any definition, and it was approved under the original Dec. 2018 ordinance, which
  had no MW tiers. The community-scale/utility-scale split did not exist until Feb. 2025.
  `[gov-record]` See "Precedent check" below for the 2026 Comprehensive Plan language, which does
  address the question.
- **Inter-project spacing: N/A for either tier.** No numeric spacing rule exists; proximity to
  other facilities is only a discretionary factor the Board may weigh when granting a waiver for a
  *utility-scale* CUP specifically (§ 4-608(B)(4)) — not an independent requirement, and not
  extended to community-scale by its own terms. `[gov-record]`

**Official whose interpretation would settle the cap ambiguity:** Horace H. Wade III, Director,
Surry County Department of Planning & Community Development — (757) 294-5210.

## Cap status: acreage reconciliation (recomputed 2026-09-23)

**The county's own figure: 8,260 acres used, leaving 2,435 acres under the 7% cap.** From the
Energy Amendment appendix of the 2026 Comprehensive Plan (amendment adopted April 6, 2023; PDF
p. 188): "three utility-scale solar projects have been built or approved for construction in Surry
County, totaling 8,260 acres, or 5.41% of developable land." The same appendix: "the County has
152,780 acres of developable land." `[gov-record]` —
[DocumentCenter/View/2159](https://www.surrycountyva.gov/DocumentCenter/View/2159).

Arithmetic (recomputed in code):

```
0.07 × 152,780 = 10,694.6  → 10,695 (matches ordinance)
0.10 × 152,780 = 15,278    (matches 2023 cap)
8,260 / 152,780 = 5.41%    (matches plan)
10,695 − 8,260  = 2,435 acres headroom  (8,260 / 10,695 = 77.2% of cap used)
```

Wade's late-2024 "roughly 6%" (`[press]`, Smithfield Times, above) is consistent with 5.41% of
developable land. **INFERENCE:** the press phrase meant 6% of developable land, not 6% of the cap.

```
3,905 + 672 + 1,341 = 5,918  → headroom 10,695 − 5,918 = 4,777   (gap to county figure: 2,342)
3,905 + 672 + 3,224 = 7,801  → headroom 10,695 − 7,801 = 2,894   (gap: 459)
```

## Land use taxation

**No.** Surry County has not adopted a land use value assessment ordinance under Va. Code §
58.1-3230 et seq. Virginia Tech's LUVA program lists Surry explicitly: "Surry — No use-value
assessment ordinance." `[gov-record]` — [luva.aaec.vt.edu/estimates](https://luva.aaec.vt.edu/estimates/).
Corroborated by the absence of a land-use-taxation article in Surry's own Code of Ordinances,
Chapter 16 (which has only property-tax *exemption* provisions for the elderly/disabled and farm
equipment). `[gov-record]` Since there is no program, there is no enrollment deadline.

## Why no Phase II award here

Dominion's Phase II shared solar awards (all dated 7/1/2026) include Smithfield, Suffolk,
Petersburg, Chesapeake, Norfolk, Toano, and King William — none in Surry County, despite Surry
having more existing utility-scale solar capacity than any of the other two counties in this set.
`[gov-record]` — [Dominion Shared Solar Program page](https://www.dominionenergy.com/virginia/renewable-energy-programs/shared-solar-program).

**No public-record explanation found stating a cause.** What was checked:

- Dominion's program page states only "Capacity will be awarded on a first-come basis" — no
  locality-based allocation, quota, or exclusion process is described anywhere in the program
  rules. `[gov-record]`
- No local news coverage (Smithfield Times and others searched) discusses Surry's absence from
  the Phase II award list specifically.
- SCC's shared-solar program overview page references docket **PUR-2024-00122**, but that page
  carries no locality-level detail; an interactive query at
  [scc.virginia.gov/docketsearch](https://scc.virginia.gov/docketsearch) was not completed for
  this specific question.

**One factor worth flagging as context, not a confirmed cause — and now weaker than previously
stated:** a shared-solar facility (state-capped at 5 MW) would be classified under Surry's
ordinance as a "community-scale solar energy facility," still requiring a CUP in M-1/M-2 zoning
only (see "Does this apply at 5 MW" above). Whether it would also count toward the 7%/10,695-acre
cap is itself **ambiguous** on the ordinance's own text (the cap sentence names only
"utility-scale," not community-scale), though the 2026 Comprehensive Plan counts community-scale
projects against it. As of 2026-09-23, the county's own figure leaves **2,435 acres** of
headroom, far more than a 5 MW project needs; see "Cap status" above. **INFERENCE:** cap
exhaustion is therefore not a plausible cause. The M-1/M-2 rezoning-plus-CUP path remains a
possible friction point, but no record links it to any Phase II applicant.

   implementation filing may list applications received by locality/substation.
2. A direct records request to Dominion's Shared Solar Program office
   (sharedsolar@dominionenergy.com) asking whether any Subscriber Organization ever filed a
   completed application for a Surry County-sited facility, and its disposition.
3. Surry County Planning & Community Development (Horace Wade, (757) 294-5210) for a current
