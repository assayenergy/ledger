## Solar ordinance

- **Definition, Sec. 2-1002: single tier, exclusion-based, no MW floor.** A "utility scale solar
  energy facility" is a facility that "(a) Generates electricity from sunlight… to include energy
  storage" and "(c) Does not meet any of the following criteria: has a disturbance zone equal to or
  less than two (2) acres, is mounted on or over a building or parking lot or other previously
  disturbed area, or utilizes integrated PV only." `[gov-record]` — Municode (above). The same text
  appears as adopted in the [May 18, 2023 BOS minutes](https://lfweb.isleofwightus.net/WebLink/DocView.aspx?id=1685&dbid=1&repo=CountyAdministration).
  - No small, community or shared tier exists, and no minimum MW appears anywhere in the code.
    `[gov-record]` (Sec. 2-1002 and Articles III–V searched).
  - **UNVERIFIED:** how a ground-mounted facility disturbing 2 acres or less is classified; no
    provision says.
  - **UNVERIFIED:** what "disturbance zone" means. It is not defined in Sec. 2-1002.
- **Permit type:** **Conditional Use Permit.** Solar is a "Utility service/major" use (Sec.
  3-4000). The use table (Sec. 4-1005) lists it as **C** in RAC, RR, VC, NC, SE, SR, UR, LC, GC,
  LI, GI, GIC, PDR, PDMH, PDCP and PDMX, and as **P** (by right) in PD-IP. `[gov-record]` —
  Municode, Sec. 4-1005.
  - **Siting limits, Sec. 5-5003.M.4(i)–(j):** facilities "shall not be located within the limits
    of the County's Development Service Districts" and "must be wholly located within a two-mile
    radius of existing substations and/or high voltage transmission lines." `[gov-record]`
  - **Process, Sec. 1-1017:** a Planning Commission hearing and recommendation, then a Board
    hearing and decision. A CUP terminates if the use is not established "within two (2) years
    from the date of approval" (Sec. 1-1017.M.1). `[gov-record]`
- **Setbacks, Sec. 5-5003.M.4(d), verbatim:** *"All equipment, accessory structures and operations
  associated with a large solar energy system shall be setback at least seventy-five (75) feet from
  all property lines and at least one hundred, one hundred and twenty-five (125) feet from any
  residential parcels, and two hundred and fifty (250) feet from any structure with documented
  historical significance, a cemetery, a cultural resource, or a school. New substations, connector
  stations, inverters and transformers shall be set back at least two hundred and fifty (250) feet
  from the property line of any non-participating property. Final setbacks for shall be determined
  by equipment decibel level specifications."* It continues: *"ii. Setbacks shall not be required
  along property lines adjacent to other parcels which are part of the solar energy system"*.
  `[gov-record]` —
  [Sec. 5-5003 on Municode](https://library.municode.com/va/isle_of_wight_county/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=APXBZO_ARTVSUUSRE_S5-5003SUUSRECIUSTY)
  (Supp. 89), captured 2026-09-23.
  - **Measured from: property lines.** The 75 ft runs from all property lines, the residential
    figure from "residential parcels", and the equipment setback from the non-participating
    property line. No provision measures from a dwelling.
  - **The residential distance is AMBIGUOUS on the text.** The code reads "one hundred, one
    hundred and twenty-five (125)"; staff read it as 125 ft (Oct. 2023 staff report: "minimum
    setbacks of 75 feet from adjacent property lines and 125 feet from residential use parcels…
    250-foot setback requirement for all inverters." `[gov-record]` —
    [CUP-3-23 staff report](https://isleofwight.novusagenda.com/agendapublic/CoverSheet.aspx?ItemID=4448&MeetingID=331)).
- **Buffers, Sec. 5-5003.M.4(e):** a 50-ft bufferyard along public roads ("three (3), staggered
  rows of trees and shrubs") and a 50-ft bufferyard where the site abuts residential parcels.
  `[gov-record]` **INFERENCE:** there is no separate right-of-way setback beyond the 75-ft
  property-line setback.
- **Other standards, Sec. 5-5003.M.4:**
  - (b) height "shall not exceed fifteen (15) feet"
  - (c) noise limited to 60 dBA at non-participating property lines
  - (q) on-site energy storage "shall require a separate conditional use permit"
  - `[gov-record]`

### Countywide cap

**Sec. 5-5003.M.4.k:** "The total acres of prime farm soils and soils of statewide importance
that may be within the fence of a utility scale solar facility pursuant to issued and active
CUPs shall be limited to a maximum of two percent (2%)… or two thousand four hundred forty-six
(2,446) acres." `[gov-record]` — Municode; the section history note ends "5-8-23; 5-18-23."

- **Adoption:** May 18, 2023. The minutes read "The motion was adopted (4-1)". `[gov-record]` —
  [BOS minutes 5/18/2023](https://lfweb.isleofwightus.net/WebLink/DocView.aspx?id=1685&dbid=1&repo=CountyAdministration);
  `[press]` — [Smithfield Times, May 22, 2023](https://www.smithfieldtimes.com/2023/05/22/isle-of-wight-approves-2-cap-on-solar-farm-acreage/).
- **Still in force in 2026:** the June 18, 2026 battery-storage redline of Sec. 5-5003.M carries
  item k unchanged. `[gov-record]` — CivicClerk meeting 381
  ([API endpoint](https://isleofwightcova.api.civicclerk.com/v1/Meetings/381)).
- **Scope:** the cap has no MW or acreage floor, and it applies to any "utility scale solar
  facility." **INFERENCE:** it therefore reaches a 5 MW project, and it counts prime and
  statewide-importance soils inside the fence, not total acreage.
- **Grandfathering is not in the ordinance text.** No effective-date or pending-application clause
  was found in the adopted text. Staff are reported saying the cap "would not apply to any project
  that's submitted a conditional use permit application prior to the date of the ordinance's
  adoption." `[press]` — Smithfield Times, May 22, 2023 (above). **UNVERIFIED** in a primary
  record.

**Tally, with arithmetic.** No official county ledger of acreage against the cap was found.
Press figures:

```
2% × 122,296 prime/statewide-importance acres = 2,445.9 → 2,446   (base: [press] Tidewater News, 2023-05-09)
May 2023:  1,759 ac  → 1,759 / 2,446 = 71.9% of cap              ([press] Smithfield Times, 2023-05-22)
Oct 2023:  2,208 ac  → 2,208 / 2,446 = 90.3% of cap; 2,208 / 122,296 = 1.81%   ([press] 2023-10-26)
Aug 2025:  "2,446 acres, all of which is now spoken for"          ([press] 2025-08-25)
```

Sources: [Smithfield Times, Oct. 26, 2023](https://www.smithfieldtimes.com/2023/10/26/iw-planners-split-6-4-on-courthouse-highway-solar-farm/);
[Smithfield Times, Aug. 25, 2025](https://www.smithfieldtimes.com/2025/08/25/isle-of-wight-supervisors-vote-3-1-to-approve-permit-extension-for-stalled-solar-farm/).

**Caveats on the tally:**
- The press counts "cumulative acreage of existing and proposed solar farms." The ordinance counts
  in-fence prime soils under "issued and active CUPs." The two metrics differ.
- The May 18, 2023 minutes record two other figures, 2,200 acres and 2,619 acres, that do not
  reconcile with the press figures. `[gov-record]`
- **UNVERIFIED:** current headroom.

**State law:** HB 711 / SB 347 took effect July 1, 2026 and cover projects of 1 MW or more. The
Virginia Association of Counties "contends the laws will effectively invalidate percentage-based
solar caps like those adopted by Isle of Wight." `[press]` —
[Smithfield Times, May 26, 2026](https://www.smithfieldtimes.com/news/bills-barring-localities-from-banning-solar-farms-become-law-6e8f5c3d).
**UNVERIFIED:** the statute's effect on Sec. 5-5003.M.4.k. The statutory text was not read, and
no county response was found.

### Spacing

- **No numeric inter-project spacing rule.** `[gov-record]` (Sec. 5-5003.M.4 searched)
- **Discretionary standard:** CUP findings include that the use "will not result in a
  multiplicity or saturation of similar uses in the same general neighborhood" (Sec.
  1-1017.H.7). `[gov-record]` Staff invoked it to recommend denial of a 3 MW project (Orbit Road,
  below), citing other solar sites half a mile and 0.6 miles away. `[press]` —
  [Smithfield Times, Aug. 16, 2024](https://www.smithfieldtimes.com/2024/08/16/iw-supervisors-approve-10th-solar-farm-table-two-others/).

## Does this apply at 5 MW

**APPLIES.** A 5 MW ground-mounted project disturbing more than 2 acres is a "utility scale solar
energy facility" under Sec. 2-1002. It needs a CUP in every district except PD-IP, it cannot be
sited inside a Development Service District, and it is subject to the setbacks above.
`[gov-record]` **INFERENCE:** it also counts toward the 2%/2,446-acre cap. That cap is reported
fully allocated, and HB 711 / SB 347 may override it; both points are **UNVERIFIED** (see
"Countywide cap").

## Precedent check (added 2026-09-23)

**Verdict: PRECEDENT_SUPPORTS**, qualified. The Board has granted CUPs for three solar
facilities of 2–3 MW under the same single-tier ordinance: Nuby Run (2 MW, 5–0, 2022), Courthouse
Hwy Solar 1 (3 MW, 5–0, 2023) and Orbit Road (3 MW, 3–1, 2024). The one reviewed record on the
cap for a small project, Courthouse Hwy, does not mention the cap. Two things are unresolved:
whether a post-cap application of this size would count against a cap the press reports as fully
allocated, and whether the approvals so far were grandfathered.
