## Solar ordinance

- **Adopted:** February 17, 2022 — "An Ordinance to Amend and Reenact... Article XXIII, Solar and
  Battery Facilities, Supplementary Use Regulations," signed by Board Chairman Susan Seward.
  `[gov-record]` — [Adopted Solar Ordinance, Feb. 2022](https://sussexcountyva.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Adopted-Solar-Ordniance-Feb.-2022.pdf) (read directly, page 18 signature page).
- **Most recent amendment:** Zoning Text Amendment #2026-03 (accessory Battery Energy Storage
  System siting standards) went to a joint Board of Supervisors/Planning Commission public
  hearing June 30, 2026. The Board Action Form retrieved is an unsigned/blank voting sheet, so the
  outcome of the vote is not confirmed from this document. `[gov-record]` —
  [Board Action Form, ZTA #2026-03](https://sussexcountyva.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Board-Action-Form-Zoning-Text-Amendment_.pdf); [draft ordinance text](https://sussexcountyva.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Accessory-BESS-Draft-Ordinance-6_30_26_CLEAN-002.pdf).
  **Unverified:** whether it passed — the July 2026 Board of Supervisors minutes (not yet posted
  as of this writing) at [sussexcountyva.gov/agendas-and-minutes](https://sussexcountyva.gov/agendas-and-minutes/) would confirm.
- **By-right vs. SUP/CUP:** Utility-scale solar requires a **Conditional Use Permit (CUP)** in
  General Agricultural (A-1), Limited Industrial (I-1), and General Industrial (I-2) districts;
  not permitted at all in Residential Multi-Family (R-1). Medium-scale solar is by-right in I-1
  and I-2 only. Sec. 16-403(c). `[gov-record]`
- **Setback from residences:** **300 feet** from a dwelling (also 150 ft from adjacent property
  lines and 150 ft from public rights-of-way — whichever is more restrictive controls). The
  Planning Commission or Board may require up to **400 feet** where topography or structure
  height increases visual impact. Sec. 16-406(d)(1)–(2). `[gov-record]`
- **Acreage/percentage cap:** **None county-wide.** The ordinance sets only per-project standards:
  minimum facility area of 100 acres, maximum 65% acreage-coverage ratio per project. Sec.
  16-406(b)(1)–(2). `[gov-record]`
- **Inter-project spacing rule:** **None.** The ordinance contains no minimum-distance requirement
  between separate solar projects. `[gov-record]`

All of the above verified directly against the ordinance text: [Sussex County Code, Appendix B
(Zoning), Article XXIII, §§16-401–16-410](https://sussexcountyva.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Adopted-Solar-Ordniance-Feb.-2022.pdf).

## Does this apply at 5 MW

**AMBIGUOUS.**

Sussex's ordinance (Sec. 16-1, Definitions) defines solar facilities by size tier: `[gov-record]`

- *Solar facility, small-scale*: "a ground-mounted facility... on a facility area of less than one
  acre or having a rated capacity of less than 250 kW alternating current."
- *Solar facility, medium-scale*: "a ground mounted facility... on a facility area of between one to
  ten acres or having a rated capacity of between 250 kW to one megawatt (MW) alternating current."
- *Solar facility, utility-scale*: "a ground mounted facility... on a facility area of not less than
  65 acres based upon the 100 acre minimum area requirement contained in Section 16-406(b)(1)...
  approximately equivalent to a rated capacity of about one megawatt (MW) alternating current or
  greater."
- *Solar facility, shared*: "a facility that generates electricity from sunlight... that will be
  part of an investor-owned utility's shared solar pilot program. A shared solar facility does not
  exceed five megawatts (5 MW) alternating current, serves at least three subscribers, has at
  least 40 percent of its capacity subscribed by customers with subscriptions of 25 kilowatts or
  less... This facility type is a subset of either rooftop, small-scale, medium-scale, or
  utility-scale solar facility." — this is the exact category Dominion's Phase II shared-solar
  awards fall into.

**A 5 MW AC / ~30–40 acre project does not cleanly fit any tier that has an assigned zoning
permission.** It exceeds medium-scale's ceiling (10 acres / 1 MW) but falls short of utility-scale's
floor (100-acre minimum facility area, per Sec. 16-406(b)(1) — the operative test, with the "~1 MW
or greater" language being descriptive, not the controlling threshold). And while "solar facility,
shared" is explicitly defined by MW (≤5 MW) with no acreage floor — matching this size exactly —
**the permitted-use table itself (Sec. 16-403(c)) lists only four rows: Multi-family shared,
Medium-scale, Utility-scale, and (implicitly) Rooftop/small-scale by-right. "Solar facility,
shared" has no corresponding row and no assigned CUP/by-right status anywhere in the zoning
text.** `[gov-record]`

Since Sussex has no county-wide acreage/percentage cap and no inter-project spacing rule at all
(see "Solar ordinance" above), those two questions are moot here regardless of tier — only the
CUP-requirement question is ambiguous.

**Official whose interpretation would settle it:** Beverly Walkup, Planning Director, Sussex
County Planning and Zoning Department — (434) 246-1042, bwalkup@sussexcountyva.gov. `[gov-record]`
— [Planning and Zoning Staff](https://sussexcountyva.gov/department/planning-and-zoning-department/planning-and-zoning-staff/).

## Cap status

There is no codified cap to "reach." The county's own Planning & Zoning Department nonetheless
tracks cumulative solar land use informally: as of an April 17, 2026 snapshot, **approved solar
facilities covered 2.52% of county acreage**, with **pending applications covering an additional
3.83%**. `[gov-record]` — [Sussex County Solar/BESS Information page](https://sussexcountyva.gov/department/planning-and-zoning-department/solar-energy-facility-battery-storage-facilities-information/).
This is informational tracking, not a legal ceiling.

## Land use taxation

**No.** Sussex County has not adopted a land use value assessment ordinance under Va. Code §
58.1-3230 et seq. Virginia Tech's statewide Land Use Value Assessment program lists Sussex
explicitly as: "Sussex – No use-value assessment ordinance." `[gov-record]` (state-administered
program of record) — [luva.aaec.vt.edu/estimates](https://luva.aaec.vt.edu/estimates/). Since
there is no program, there is no enrollment deadline.

## Why no Phase II award here

Dominion's Phase II shared solar awards (all dated 7/1/2026) include Smithfield, Suffolk,
Petersburg, Chesapeake, Norfolk, Toano, and King William — none in Sussex County. `[gov-record]`
— [Dominion Shared Solar Program page](https://www.dominionenergy.com/virginia/renewable-energy-programs/shared-solar-program).

**No public explanation found.** What was checked:

- Dominion's program page states only "Capacity will be awarded on a first-come basis" — no
  locality-specific criteria, no mention of Sussex, substation limits, or a moratorium.
  `[gov-record]`
- Sussex's solar ordinance was already in force (Feb. 2022) well before the Phase II application
  window, so an "ordinance didn't exist" explanation is ruled out. `[gov-record]`
- No SCC filing, no Sussex County planning/BOS record, and no local news coverage found stating
  that any Subscriber Organization applied for Phase II capacity in Sussex County, or citing a
  distribution-capacity constraint specific to the county.
- Unlike Surry and Southampton (see their files), Sussex has **no acreage/percentage cap** that
  could plausibly have been exhausted — so that theory doesn't apply here either.

**Document or contact that would settle it:**

1. Dominion's Phase II interconnection/distribution planning studies for the Sussex County
   service area, or its [hosting capacity map](https://www.dominionenergy.com/hostingcapacity).

3. SCC docket **PUR-2020-00125** ("Shared Solar Program") at
